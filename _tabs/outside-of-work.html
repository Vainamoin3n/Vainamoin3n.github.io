Playing & making games

The game, the team, and one more idea to try.

03

Improvisational theatre

“Yes, and…” is a good place to start.

I studied long-form improv at Improv Helsinki. You build a character and a story without knowing where the next scene will go. That takes listening, trust, and a willingness to look a little silly.

On stage

You pay attention to what your scene partner gives you: their words, their expression, their energy. Then you add something they can build on. You cannot plan the whole story in advance.

What stays with me

Improv has helped me with storytelling, confidence, and listening. It is also a reminder that someone else's idea can take you somewhere you would never have reached on your own.
